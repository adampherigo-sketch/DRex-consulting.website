What We Work On

Support built around the problem.

The work looks different depending on the project or the challenge in front of you. These four areas describe where DRex Consulting usually helps — most engagements borrow from more than one.

Start with the problem. The plan follows.

01

Strategy & Planning

Turn a goal, idea, or open question into a clear direction. We help define what matters most, map the path to get there, and set priorities you can actually work from.

Example areas of support

  • Strategic planning
  • Goal clarification
  • Project roadmaps
  • Priority setting
  • Decision support

02

Project Development

Take a project from a rough concept to an organized, workable plan. We help shape the structure, sequence the work, and get it ready for implementation.

Example areas of support

  • Idea development
  • Project organization
  • Implementation planning
  • Project structure
  • Milestone planning

03

Problem Solving

Work through a challenge that needs more than a quick answer. We help locate the real roadblock, weigh the options, and bring an outside perspective to the decision.

Example areas of support

  • Identifying roadblocks
  • Process review
  • Alternative solutions
  • Decision frameworks
  • Outside perspective

04

Organizational Support

Strengthen the systems and habits that keep good work moving. We look at how work flows, how people communicate, and how planning happens day to day.

Example areas of support

  • Workflow improvements
  • Communication structure
  • Planning systems
  • Operational organization
  • Process documentation

Not sure which one you need? That's okay.

Most projects touch more than one area. Pick the closest fit, or just describe the situation and we'll sort out where it lands.

How the work goes

Listen, Understand, Plan, Act, Adjust

Most engagements move through the same five steps. The order stays the same; how long each one takes depends on the work.

  1. Step 1

    Listen

    We hear the full picture — context, constraints, and goals.

    ↓
  2. Step 2

    Understand

    We find the real problem beneath the surface symptoms.

    ↓
  3. Step 3

    Plan

    We map a practical path with clear priorities and steps.

    ↓
  4. Step 4

    Act

    We put the plan in motion and keep the work organized.

    ↓
  5. Step 5

    Adjust

    We review what's working and refine as things change.
